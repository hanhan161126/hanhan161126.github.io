Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Hermes Uploader is a personal application used solely by its owner (hanhan161126@gmail.com) to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data accessed

The app uses YouTube API Services with the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly to upload videos and read basic channel information (channel name and ID).

Data use and storage

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's local computer. No data is collected from other users, sold, or shared with third parties.

Third-party services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
